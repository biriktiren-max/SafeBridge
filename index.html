Direct wallet-to-wallet transfer for POL, USDT, USDT.e and PAXG on Polygon Mainnet.

How to connect

Use your own wallet. SafeBridge never asks for your secret recovery phrase. You approve the connection and each transfer inside the wallet.

  1. Install a wallet below, or on mobile tap Open so this page loads inside that wallet.
  2. Unlock the wallet and switch the network to Polygon Mainnet.
  3. Tap Connect and approve the request. Your address shows at the top.
  4. Enter the recipient, amount, and asset, then tap Send Instant Transfer and confirm in the wallet.
